FAQ

  • No sound? Check that Settings → Audio is on, and check your silent switch and media volume.
  • Where is my progress? Only on your device. Before changing phones or deleting the app, export a save code from Settings → Save & Storage.
  • A workshop won't open? Close other memory-heavy apps and try again; the Jiangnan Scene Workshop takes about ten seconds to build its first scene.
  • Found a bug? Send us your device model, iOS version and a screenshot.
