常见问题

  • 进度存在哪里？只存在你的设备上。暂不支持云存档或换机迁移，卸载会清除进度。
  • 想从头再来？打开「设置」，点「删档重来」，确认后会回到开场。
  • 没有声音？请检查设置里的「声音」开关，以及手机侧边的静音键。
  • 画面卡顿或发烫？可在设置中打开「减少动态」，或切到「棋盘」档位减少远景。

FAQ

  • Where is my progress stored? Only on your device. There is no cloud save or device transfer yet, and deleting the app clears your progress.
  • Start over? Open Settings and tap Erase and restart; after you confirm, the game returns to the opening.
  • No sound? Check the Sound switch in Settings and your phone's silent switch.
  • Stutter or heat? Turn on Reduce motion in Settings, or switch to the Board view to draw less scenery.
