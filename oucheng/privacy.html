偶成 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 28 日

偶成（以下称"本应用"）由个人开发者陈嘉娴开发。我们尊重并保护你的隐私。

一、我们不收集你的个人信息

本应用无需注册或登录，不包含广告、统计或追踪代码，不收集、不上传、不出售任何个人信息或使用数据。

二、你的作品只保存在你的设备上

你写下的诗词、草稿、文集、印章与设置，全部保存在本设备的应用存储中。卸载本应用会一并删除这些数据。如需保留或迁移，请使用"墨迹"中的 JSON 备份功能。备份文件由你自行保管。

三、网络访问

iOS 版偶成的全部功能都在本地完成，所需字体已随应用安装，不进行任何网络请求。

网页版为显示排版字体，会从公共字体分发网络（fonts.loli.net、gstatic.loli.net、cdn.jsdelivr.net）加载字体文件。这些服务按常规会收到你设备的 IP 地址和浏览器标识，用于传输文件；偶成不会读取、记录或保存这些信息，也不会把你的作品或草稿发给它们。

四、分享与导出

当你导出海报或备份时，本应用调用系统分享面板。之后数据发往何处由你选择，适用你所选应用的隐私政策。

五、儿童

本应用不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

六、政策变更

如本政策有变更，我们会更新本页面并修改生效日期。

七、联系我们

联系邮箱：tardiselysee@gmail.com

Ou Cheng Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

Ou Cheng ("the app") is developed by an individual developer, Chen Jiaxian.

1. We do not collect personal data

The app requires no account and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code. We do not collect, upload or sell any personal or usage data.

2. Your poems stay on your device

Poems, drafts, collections, seals and settings are stored only in the app's storage on this device and are deleted if you delete the app. Use the JSON backup in Works to keep or move them; you control the backup file.

3. Network access

The iOS app runs entirely on your device. Its fonts are bundled with the app, and it makes no network requests.

The web version loads typesetting fonts from public font CDNs (fonts.loli.net, gstatic.loli.net, cdn.jsdelivr.net). These services receive your IP address and user agent as part of delivering the files; Ou Cheng does not read, log or store this information, and never sends your poems or drafts to them.

4. Sharing and export

Exports use the system share sheet; where the data goes next is your choice and is governed by the destination's policy.

5. Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
