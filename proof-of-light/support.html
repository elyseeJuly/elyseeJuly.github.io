常见问题

  • 没有声音？iPhone 侧面的静音键打开时，游戏不会出声。也可以在「设置 → 声音」里调整音乐和音效。
  • 怎么换手机？旧设备「设置 → 存档 → 导出存档」复制存档码；新设备「导入存档」粘贴后点「载入」。
  • 想从头再玩？「设置 → 存档 → 重新开始」，需要确认两次。
  • 卡关了？顶部的指引栏会列出每一步。停得久了会逐级给出提示，最后一级可以「演示一次 / 一键对准」。日志里的线索曼荼罗也会告诉你下一项校准去哪里找。
  • 天体是真的吗？具名恒星的位置、自行和视差来自真实星表；星云与星系按真实坐标放置，形态由代码参照真实观测重建；背景星场为程序合成。POL-00、HSO-7、HSO-9、OGLE-BLG-0613 等少数天体为游戏虚构，游戏内已标注。

Proof of Light · Support

FAQ

  • No sound? The game is silent while the iPhone's mute switch is on. Music and effects can also be adjusted in Settings → Sound.
  • Moving to a new phone? On the old device, Settings → Save → Export copies a save code; on the new one, Import, paste and tap Load.
  • Start over? Settings → Save → Restart, confirmed twice.
  • Stuck? The guide bar at the top lists every step. If you pause for a while it offers stronger hints, up to a one-tap demo. The clue map in the log shows where the next calibration is.
  • Is the sky real? Named stars use catalogue positions, motions and parallaxes; nebulae and galaxies sit at their true coordinates, with shapes rebuilt in code from real observations; background star fields are procedural. POL-00, HSO-7, HSO-9, OGLE-BLG-0613 and a few others are fictional and labelled in the game.
