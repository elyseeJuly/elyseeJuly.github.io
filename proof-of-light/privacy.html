宇宙观测者 · 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 3 日

宇宙观测者（Proof of Light，以下称"本应用"）由个人开发者陈嘉娴开发。

一、我们不收集你的个人信息

本应用无需注册或登录，不包含广告、统计、崩溃上报或追踪代码，不收集、不上传、不出售任何个人信息或使用数据。

二、存档只保存在你的设备上

你的观测进度、证据、预测和设置只保存在本设备的应用存储中。卸载本应用会一并删除它们。需要换机时，请在「设置 → 存档」里导出存档码，存档码由你自行保管。

三、网络访问

iOS 版的所有画面、音乐和音效都由代码在本机实时生成，不进行任何网络请求。

网页版托管在 Cloudflare。和所有网站一样，托管服务在传输文件时会收到你设备的 IP 地址和浏览器标识；本应用不会读取、记录或保存这些信息。

四、外部链接

设置页里的「隐私政策」和「帮助与反馈」会用系统浏览器打开本网站，适用网站托管方的常规政策。

五、儿童

本应用不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

六、政策变更

如有变更，我们会更新本页面并修改生效日期。

七、联系我们

联系邮箱：tardiselysee@gmail.com

Proof of Light · Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

Proof of Light ("the app") is developed by an individual developer, Chen Jiaxian.

1. We do not collect personal data

The app requires no account and contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking code. We do not collect, upload or sell any personal or usage data.

2. Your save stays on your device

Your progress, evidence, predictions and settings are stored only in the app's storage on this device and are deleted if you delete the app. To move to a new phone, export a save code from Settings → Save; you keep that code.

3. Network access

The iOS app generates every image, note and sound on your device and makes no network requests.

The web version is hosted on Cloudflare. As with any website, the host receives your IP address and user agent while delivering the files; the app does not read, log or store them.

4. External links

The Privacy Policy and Help links in Settings open this website in your system browser.

5. Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
