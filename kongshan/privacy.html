空山息木 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 3 日

空山息木（以下称"本游戏"）由个人开发者陈嘉娴开发。

一、我们不收集你的个人信息

本游戏无需注册或登录，不包含广告、内购、统计或追踪代码，不收集、不上传、不出售任何个人信息或游戏数据。

二、进度只保存在你的设备上

山林、主线进度、观察簿和设置都保存在本设备的应用存储里。卸载本游戏会一并删除这些数据；也可以在「设置 → 删档重来」中主动清除。本游戏暂不提供云存档。

三、网络访问

iOS 版空山息木的画面、音乐和全部玩法都在本地生成，不进行任何网络请求。只有当你在设置里点开「隐私政策」或「帮助与反馈」时，系统浏览器才会打开本页面。

网页版托管在 Cloudflare，加载页面时 Cloudflare 按常规会处理你的 IP 地址和浏览器标识，用于传输文件；本游戏不读取、不记录这些信息。

四、儿童

本游戏不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

五、政策变更

如本政策有变更，我们会更新本页面并修改生效日期。

六、联系我们

联系邮箱：tardiselysee@gmail.com

Kongshan Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

Kongshan ("the game") is developed by an individual developer, Chen Jiaxian.

1. We do not collect personal data

The game requires no account and contains no advertising, in-app purchases, analytics or tracking code. We do not collect, upload or sell any personal or gameplay data.

2. Your progress stays on your device

Your valley, story progress, field notes and settings are stored only in the app's storage on this device. Deleting the app deletes them; you can also clear them under Settings → Erase and restart. There is no cloud save.

3. Network access

The iOS game generates its graphics, music and gameplay on your device and makes no network requests. Only if you tap Privacy Policy or Support in Settings does the system browser open this page.

The web version is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes your IP address and user agent to deliver the files. The game does not read or log this information.

4. Children

The game collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

5. Changes

Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
